Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 July 2026

Westward Dash (齐天大冲刺) collects no personal data. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. It makes no network requests to Mars Paladin LLC — we operate no servers and receive nothing from your copy of the game.

What stays on your device

Save files, settings, player profiles, unlock progress and local game logs are written to your own computer and stay there. Deleting the game's save folder removes them permanently. We have no copy of them and no way to obtain one.

Playing through Steam

If you play the Steam version, some features are provided by Valve rather than by us: achievements, and online multiplayer, which connects players peer-to-peer through Steam's networking. Using those features means Valve processes the data it needs to run them — such as your Steam ID and which lobby you are in — under Valve's privacy policy. We do not receive that data. Single-player works fully offline.

Children

Because the game collects nothing from anyone, it collects nothing from children.

Changes

If a future update ever collects anything — an opt-in crash report, for example — this page will be updated before that update ships, and the change will be disclosed in the game's Steam store listing.
